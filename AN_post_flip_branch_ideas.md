So the read: **identity** (Goldstar Federation vs Second Confederation) → **posture** (peace / coercion / conquest) → **method of growth** (diplomacy / claims / covert ops / open war) → **great-power alignment** (Axis / Allies / neither) → **capstone ending**. Five different "how America became a fascist hemispheric power" stories, all reconverging at `a_golden_west`.

## Identity resolution

### The Goldstar Federation
*Already has mechanical shape: dynamic modifier tied to remaining faction leader, creates a "commonwealth"-style faction, sets cosmetic tag `USA_goldstar`.*

**Theming:** the *technocratic* fascist outcome — the old Union survives, but reorganized as a hierarchical federation bound together by a national mythology of the five-pointed star (unity, order, industry). Not nostalgic, forward-looking — "we didn't tear the country apart, we rebuilt it into something stronger."

**Desc idea:** something like — *"The old republic proved too soft to hold itself together, but it need not be discarded. Bound by the Goldstar and administered with a firm, unifying hand, the several states and territories of the Union can be forged into a single federation — strong where the old order was weak."*

### The Second Confederation
*Sets `USA_american_nationalist` cosmetic tag; requires `the_gettysburg_debate`.*

**Theming:** the reactionary/traditionalist outcome — explicitly reclaims Confederate/agrarian-elite iconography, repackaged as "American Nationalism" rather than sectionalism. More overtly revanchist than Goldstar Federation.

**Desc idea:** *"Let the old Union die where it stood. What rises in its place will not apologize for what it is — a nation of blood, soil, and unbroken tradition, finally free to call itself what it always was."*

## National posture (pick one — the mutual-exclusion trio)

### Our Safety Before Yours *(icon: GER protectorate focus)*
**Theming:** the "protection racket" option — coercive hegemony without outright annexation. Guarantees wrapped around garrison rights, resource concessions, basing rights.

**Desc idea:** *"Our neighbors' safety is inseparable from our own — and they should be made to understand that such safety comes at a price. Garrisons, concessions, and a firm hand in their affairs are a small toll for the protection only we can offer."*

**Effect ideas:** opinion bonus with weaker neighbors, a claim/demand-territory-style decision category unlocked toward Mexico/Central America, maybe a "protectorate" wargoal type usable short of full war.

### The Sun Has Already Set *(icon: SWE decline-of-old-world focus)*
**Theming:** explicitly framed as European decline — "their empires are dying, ours is rising." Opens the conquest branch.

**Desc idea:** *"London and Paris cling to empires they can no longer afford to hold. Let them. While the old powers exhaust themselves, the hemisphere lies open before us — and a nation unwilling to seize its moment does not deserve one."*

**Effect ideas:** small war-support/PP boost, unlocks the Gulf/Canal/Flanks war-prep trio, maybe a justify-war-goal discount vs. American-continent nations.

*(Peace Guarantees already has strong flavor — no changes needed there.)*

## Growth method (off Peace Guarantees / Our Safety Before Yours)

### America's Rightful Claims *(icon: Manifest Destiny)*
**Theming:** the overt-but-still-short-of-war option — leaning on the Peace Guarantees track, this is "diplomacy backed by an unmistakable claim."

**Desc idea:** *"There is no need for apology in stating plainly what has always been true: this continent's destiny runs through Washington. We will simply say so — and let our neighbors decide how they wish to accommodate it."*

**Effect ideas:** cores/claims on contested North American states, compliance/opinion bonus framed as "at least we're honest about it" vs. the covert option.

### Golden Opportunities *(icon: NOR "lurk in the shadows")*
**Theming:** the deniable option off Our Safety Before Yours + Sun Has Already Set — coups, funded factions, quiet regime change instead of claims.

**Desc idea:** *"Not every opportunity needs an army behind it. A well-placed loan, a sympathetic colonel, a little patience — and governments change hands without a shot ever being fired in our name."*

**Effect ideas:** intel/operative bonuses toward Central/South American targets, chance-based "friendly coup" decisions, deniability (lower world tension cost than outright annexation).

## Sun Has Already Set → conquest trio → Golden Circle

These three currently have literal comments as placeholders — good instinct, just needs fleshing out. Suggest keeping them as *complementary, stackable* military-prep focuses (all three feed the same capstone) rather than exclusive choices:

### Secure the Gulf *(navy, Gulf of Mexico)*
**Desc idea:** *"The Gulf is an American lake in all but name. It is past time we made that fact impossible to ignore."*
**Effects:** naval bonus in Gulf sea zones, possibly a marine/invasion-prep modifier vs. Mexico/Caribbean coastlines, small dockyard or fuel bonus.

### The Rush for the Canal *(Panama)*
**Desc idea:** *"Whoever holds the isthmus holds the hinge between two oceans. We will not leave that hinge in anyone else's hands."*
**Effects:** bonus to justifying/annexing Panama-adjacent states, construction speed bonus for canal-zone infrastructure, maybe a permanent naval-transit modifier once Golden Circle is realized.

### Seize the Great Flanks *(icon: Monroe)*
**Desc idea:** *"North and south, east and west — the flanks of this continent are ours to close, and any power that tests them will find the door already shut."*
**Effects:** general invasion/deep-water combat bonus, planning bonus vs. American-continent targets specifically.

### Realize the Golden Circle *(icon: Phoenix — matches your existing "Golden Circle empire" endgame)*
**Desc idea:** *"From the ashes of the old republic rises something larger — a Golden Circle binding the continent under a single, unbreakable order. What was fractured is now whole, and what is whole cannot easily be broken again."*
**Effects (per your comment):** mass cores across North/Central America, a major national spirit that blocks joining other factions (thematically: "we don't need allies, we are the hemisphere"), possibly a stability/war-support boost proportional to controlled American states.

### No Place for Traitors *(side branch, spy agency)*
Already has full effects written — just needs desc. This reads as the "purge the home front" focus that pairs with the conquest trio (you're about to go to war, so tighten internal security first).

**Desc idea:** *"An empire built abroad is worth nothing if it can be undone by whispers at home. Before we look outward, we will make certain there is no daylight left for traitors to hide in."*

## Great-power alignment trio

### Pledge for the New Order *(icon: mission to Germany — Axis)*
**Desc idea:** *"Berlin offers a partnership between equals — a New Order built on strength answering strength. There is no shame in standing beside the only powers unafraid to build a new world."*
**Effects:** relations/faction-invite bonus with Axis members, maybe research-sharing or lend-lease-style bonus, ideology popularity nudge.

→ **Renaissance and Retribution** *(icon: Global Hegemony)*
**Theming:** the classic "ride the Axis to the top, then take the top for yourself" ending — "Renaissance" (America rebuilds/ascends) + "Retribution" (settling scores, possibly even with erstwhile Axis partners once they're no longer useful).
**Desc idea:** *"An alliance is a ladder, not a destination. We will climb it as far as it takes us — and when the summit is in sight, we will remember precisely who helped us and who merely used us."*
**Effects:** major end-state spirit/decision tree toward global hegemony, possibly unlocks a "turn on the Axis" decision late-game.

### Throw in with the Old *(icon: join the Allies)*
**Desc idea:** *"The old democracies are tired, but tired is not the same as finished. We will lend our weight to the world we know rather than gamble everything on the world Berlin and Tokyo promise."*
**Effects:** relations bonus/faction access with Allied powers, lend-lease-style production bonus.

→ **Do Our Part** *(icon: "Double Victory")*
**Theming:** the irony here is rich — Double V was a real WWII campaign for victory over fascism abroad *and* racism at home; reusing that icon under a fascist USA is a pointed, uncomfortable detail worth leaning into rather than softening.
**Desc idea:** *"Every foundry, every farm, every family has a part to play — and we intend to see that every one of them plays it, whether they wish to or not."* (keep the "our part" framed as *mobilization/conscription of labor and industry*, with a knowing edge about who's actually being asked to "volunteer.")
**Effects:** war-support/production bonus, civilian-to-military conversion speed, possibly a compliance/consumer-goods trade-off (mobilizing the home front has a cost).

### The Eagle Flies Alone *(isolationism)*
**Desc idea:** *"We need Berlin's blessing as much as we need London's — which is to say, not at all. This hemisphere is ours to secure without asking permission from either."*
**Effects:** stability/non-interventionism bonus, reduced world tension from hemisphere actions, defense bonus.

→ **Imperial Roost**
**Desc idea:** *"An eagle does not share its nest. Every republic and remnant on this continent will learn to live under our wing — or not live under it at all."*
**Effects (per your comment):** big bonuses to bringing American nations into faction/overlordship, defensive bonuses — this is the "hemisphere hegemon, content to stop there" ending.

→ **A Song for the Horizon** *(needs Eagle Flies Alone AND Realize the Golden Circle — the "best of both" ending)*
**Desc idea:** *"There is no horizon we cannot reach on our own terms. The Circle is closed, the continent is ours, and what comes next answers to no one but us."*
**Effects (per your comment):** world-conquest-tier bonuses/decisions — this is the true endgame for a player who both conquered the hemisphere *and* refused to align with anyone.

## A couple of loose threads worth deciding before you finalize

- **Prerequisite bug** noted above (Pledge/Throw-in/Eagle should be OR, not AND, across the posture trio).
- Right now nothing distinguishes *which* posture (Peace/Safety/Sun) feeds into the alignment trio differently — mechanically they're identical prerequisites. If you want the postures to matter beyond flavor, consider light AI-weight or requirement nudges (e.g., Sun Has Already Set making Pledge for the New Order more likely, Peace Guarantees nudging toward Throw in with the Old) so the playthrough's early choices actually color its ending rather than just being a naming exercise.
- `USA_prudence_across_the_land`, `imperial_roost`, `realize_the_golden_circle`, `do_our_part`, and `renaissance_and_retribution` are your five recognized "capstones" for `a_golden_west` — worth double-checking that's the complete intended list, since `a_song_for_the_horizon` (arguably your biggest final flex) currently isn't in that OR check.
